# Product decisions

1. **Hybrid, not AI-only.** LLMs synthesize; deterministic logic checks objective evidence.
2. **Human final decision.** AI cannot execute a financial outcome.
3. **Explain recommendations.** Show strengths, gaps, risks, confidence, and guardrail outcome.
4. **Treat disagreement as useful signal.** AI/rules disagreement routes to human review.
5. **Graceful fallback.** The MVP works without an API key.
6. **No invented impact.** Synthetic evaluation is labeled synthetic.
