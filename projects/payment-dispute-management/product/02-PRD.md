# PRD

## Objective
Help dispute analysts synthesize evidence and determine the next action while preserving human accountability.

## P0
- queue and case workspace
- AI case summary
- evidence strengths/gaps
- risk signals
- proposed action + confidence
- deterministic guardrails
- human-review routing
- analyst final decision
- audit trail
- reproducible fallback without paid AI

## P1
Evidence-file ingestion, expert-labeled evals, RBAC, policy configuration, analyst feedback.

## Out of scope
Automatic chargeback submission, payment-network integration, production fraud detection, and unsupported claims of accuracy or impact.
